## 📊 About This Project

Strategic dashboard developed and led for a **major delivery platform** (direct competitor to iFood).

**Objective:** Consolidate 7 years of data (2018-2025) into a BI system enabling C-level executives to make data-driven decisions on retention, revenue, and customer segmentation.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Churn Structured by Lifecycle
**Finding:** 37.21% of churners had 1+ year tenure. Not early-stage abandonment, but exit after establishment attempt.  
**Action:** Health-check program at months 3, 6, and 12 to intervene before critical point.

### 2. Clear Economic Seasonality
**Finding:** Revenue peaks in May 2020 (R$ 0.5M), followed by gradual decline. MRRLost oscillates 2-10M with 2022 spike.  
**Action:** Quarterly retention planning aligned with historical seasonal periods.

### 3. Category Concentration
**Finding:** Pizzeria dominates at 26% (R$ 145M). Restaurants generate 12% of churn (140 cases), disproportionate to revenue.  
**Action:** Customized product for restaurants with adapted economic model.

### 4. Robust Payment Compliance
**Finding:** 89.35% of customers current on payments. Structured credit model and low-risk base.  
**Action:** Healthy foundation for aggressive growth strategy.

### 5. Geographic Distribution Potential
**Finding:** Manaus (298), Belo Horizonte (147), São Paulo (39). Presence across 15 segments.  
**Action:** Vertical expansion in low-penetration cities; customized regional strategy.
